Each single-source sustained uptime is duration/cycle. Two sources' union is
at most their summed active duration per cycle, regardless of staggered phase.
Thus two Ultimates have at least10% sustained downtime at the cap; their full
double boost overlaps only where both sources are active. Cold-start windows and
short-lived legacy entitlements are measured separately from sustained uptime.
Source recasts refresh the maximum remaining deadline/strength, never self-stack.
Simultaneous expiry/cast order follows the existing chronological motor.

historical purchase levels remain, with one original-curve refund above10 and
exact spendable Shard credits. Handler, bulk/Max and automated queues share the
same cap and affordability plan. The existing three paid Resonate uses per run
and Ascension's run reset remain; this feature adds no new paid readiness bypass.

clocks, passive/Tap scope and live/offline equivalence. Independent causal
controls reject old4/8s, uncapped Swift and the old logical-only Farm clock guard.
