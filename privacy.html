Lumenfall — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026

Short version: Lumenfall does not collect, store, or share any personal data, on any platform. Everything below explains exactly why.

What the game stores

Your progress (currencies, Wisps, upgrades, settings) is saved only on your own device, using standard local app storage:

  • In a browser: localStorage, tied to that browser only.
  • In the Windows or Android app: local app storage on your device.

This data never leaves your device. It is not uploaded anywhere, not backed up to any server, and not accessible to anyone but you. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data deletes it permanently.

What the game does not do

  • No account or sign-in of any kind.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting SDKs.
  • No advertising or ad networks.
  • No in-app purchases or payment processing.
  • No access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, or any other device permission.
  • No network requests at all once installed — fonts, images, and game logic are all bundled with the app, not fetched from a server.

Third parties

None. Lumenfall doesn't integrate any third-party SDK, analytics tool, or ad network.

Children's privacy

Since no data is collected from anyone, this applies equally to players of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this ever changes — for example, if a future update adds an optional online feature — this page will be updated first, with a new "last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the developer contact listed on Lumenfall's Google Play Store page.
